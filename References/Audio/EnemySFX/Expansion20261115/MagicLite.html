Free spell sounds from my Magic Spells SFX pack: one version of each of the 30 core spells, the water and blood spells, and 2 seamless loops. Fire, ice, lightning, wind, poison, light, shadow and arcane, plus the charge, the cast, the bolt in flight, the hit, shields going up and breaking, teleports, portals, summons and the spell that fizzles.

The sounds

  • Spell flow: charge, cast, bolt, impact, fizzle
  • Elements: fire cast and impact, ice cast and shatter, zap, thunder, wind, poison, holy, shadow
  • Arcane: arcane blast, enchant, rune, dispel
  • Support: heal, buff, debuff, mana, shield up, shield break
  • Movement and conjuring: teleport, portal, summon, time warp, plus spell ready
  • Water and blood: cast and impact for each
  • Loops: fire flight and arcane aura

Format

Mono WAV, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz, plus OGG Vorbis, with a manifest.json listing every file by spell.

Updates

  • v2.0: the Lite grew to 36 sounds with the water and blood spells and the 2 loops.

The full pack keeps growing, and I'll keep bringing a few of the new sounds here.

Need variation?

A fireball can go off a hundred times in one dungeon, and the same file every time starts to sound like a loop. Magic Spells SFX has 12 different versions of every spell, seamless loops for 11 elements, and Unity and Godot scripts that never repeat a clip back to back.

For the visuals, Magic Spells VFX Lite has free pixel art spell effects that match these sounds.
