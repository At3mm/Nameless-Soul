# Boss victory cues

Original procedural audio created for this project, 2026-11-08. No audio sampled from Sekiro or third-party recordings.

- BossDefeatImpact.wav: low drum/gong-like impact, 2.4 seconds.
- BossVictoryResolve.wav: layered minor harmony and pixel-like quantization, 3.8 seconds.
- BossRewardChime.wav: three bell tones, 1.6 seconds.

Rebuild with `Tools/Audio/GenerateBossVictory.ps1`. Mono PCM 16-bit, 44.1 kHz. Volume and cues are configured in `Assets/Resources/DefaultBossVictory.asset` and respect the existing menu/master audio volume.
